A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

A clearer plan starts here.

A little clarity before your journey. Keep exploring at your own pace.

Can I plan a transfer in either direction?

Yes. Choose airport to hotel, hotel to airport or hotel to another area. Round trips have a separate return date and time. Vehicle selection does not guarantee availability.

Do I need to buy a transfer to explore tours?

No. Transfers and experiences can be explored independently. Choose a category that interests you and review its details within Bosphorly.

Does this website confirm a reservation?

Not yet. The current website lets you explore and prepare a local plan. It does not send booking requests, take payments or issue confirmed reservations.

Is membership or email marketing permission required?

You can explore without joining. Campaign emails are a separate, optional choice. The proposed 10% mobile-member benefit is still a draft and does not change displayed prices.

Where can I find cancellation and refund rules?

Terms will depend on the service and licensed provider. Product-specific cancellation, refund and inclusion details must be shown before any real purchase; no universal policy is promised here.
