# Bosphorly — brand foundation

## Selected direction
The project owner selected the dark-blue logo with a white B, cyan airplane path, golden Istanbul historical shapes and aqua Bosphorus waves. Keep that as the visual source of truth.

## Application guidance
- Primary logo: `assets/brand/bosphorly-logo.png`, on dark surfaces or as supplied.
- App icon: derived symbol-only crop, for PWA testing; manually review at 32x32 and 64x64 before app-store production.
- Use consistent colors (navy, cyan, gold) and legible sans-serif typography.
- No complex poster backgrounds, fake sponsor badges or invented heritage motifs in the core app icon.
- The imagery is raster artwork; do not claim SVG/vector status. For future pro release, recreate vector artwork from approved design.
