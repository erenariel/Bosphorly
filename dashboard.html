Production readiness checklist

The public project contains a concept frontend. The following are not implemented and must be delivered before real customer use.

  • ☐ Identity and role-based partner access
  • ☐ Supplier verification and contracts
  • ☐ Hosted PCI-compliant payment checkout
  • ☐ Booking, cancellation and refund backend
  • ☐ Personal-data protection and retention policy
  • ☐ Reconciled commission and rewards ledger
  • ☐ Monitoring, audit logging and incident response
  • ☐ Final accessibility and localization review
