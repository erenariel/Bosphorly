Frequently asked questions

How requests, payment and changes work with Bosphorly. Questions? Email us.

How do I make a request?

Choose a transfer, tour or hammam and fill in the form. An email draft opens with your request and a BOS- reference code. Press Send in your email application to deliver it to Bosphorly.

How do I pay?

Nothing is paid on this website. We share the payment method when we contact you about your request.

Are the prices final?

The airport transfer to Sultanahmet has a fixed one-way price per vehicle: €60 from Istanbul Airport (IST), €65 from Sabiha Gökçen (SAW). Tour and hammam prices are the companies’ published prices; we confirm the final price for your date on email before booking.

Which currency are prices in?

Prices are set in euros. The currency menu at the top shows approximate amounts in other currencies, using the daily rates of the Central Bank of the Republic of Türkiye.

How do I change or cancel a booking?

Write to us on email with your reference code as early as possible. The cancellation terms of your booking are given on email together with the confirmation.

Who is Bosphorly?

Bosphorly is an independent request service in Istanbul, not a tour company or travel agency. We pass your request to licensed local companies and confirm the details with you on email; the service itself is provided by the licensed company.

What happens to my personal details?

Your details reach Bosphorly only when you press Send in your email app. A draft copy may stay on your device under My account, where you can delete it.
