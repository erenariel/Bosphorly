# Bosphorly — product definition

**Goal:** Make arrival and discovery of Istanbul experiences simple for travelers, and route their requests to licensed companies, who sell, deliver and collect payment. Bosphorly earns a commission from the company for each completed referral; hotels can join as referral partners. See [BUSINESS-MODEL.md](BUSINESS-MODEL.md).

## Traveler surface (mobile first)
1. Pick IST, SAW, or skip airport transfer.
2. Identify destination hotel/location with autocomplete and a confirmed route/zone.
3. See the company's approved price, or request a quote.
4. Choose passenger count, date/time, flight number and only necessary contact details.
5. Review the company (legal name, licence), inclusions, cancellation terms and price, and that payment is made to the company.
6. Send the request; receive a Bosphorly reference code.
7. Receive the company's confirmation, pay the company directly, and explore city guides and further experiences.

## Partner surfaces (desktop optimized)
Hotels view attributed referrals and their contract-defined share; companies confirm or decline requests, maintain products, prices, availability and cancellation policies, and report coded sales monthly; administrators supervise catalogs, attribution and reconciliation.

## Distinct systems
This codebase is entirely separate from KonaklamaFlow. No production data, guest lists, keys, accounts or services are shared.

## Prohibited claims at prototype stage
No actual booking/payment, confirmed referral earnings, live fare, guaranteed discounts or authorized operator affiliation.
