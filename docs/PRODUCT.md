# Bosphorly — product definition

**Goal:** Make arrival, discovery and properly disclosed booking of Istanbul experiences simple for travelers, while licensed suppliers and hotels gain an auditable intermediary channel.

## Traveler surface (mobile first)
1. Pick IST, SAW, or skip airport transfer.
2. Identify destination hotel/location with autocomplete and a confirmed route/zone.
3. See a supplier-backed price or request an accurate quote.
4. Choose passenger count, date/time, flight number and only necessary contact details.
5. Review provider, inclusions, cancellation terms and price before consent.
6. Complete hosted secure checkout or supplier-approved pay-at-hotel selection.
7. Receive actual supplier confirmation and voucher; explore city guides and further experiences.

## Partner surfaces (desktop optimized)
Hotels view attributed referrals and contract-defined earned commission; verified agencies maintain products, availability, vouchers, fulfillment and cancellation policies; administrators supervise catalogs and reconciliations.

## Distinct systems
This codebase is entirely separate from KonaklamaFlow. No production data, guest lists, keys, accounts or services are shared.

## Prohibited claims at prototype stage
No actual booking/payment, confirmed referral earnings, live fare, guaranteed discounts or authorized operator affiliation.
