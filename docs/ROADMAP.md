# Bosphorly roadmap

## Stage 0 — Public transparent prototype (current)
Mobile-first arrival planner and supplier research links, public product vision, synthetic partner dashboard and basic Istanbul content. No real transaction capability.

## Stage 1 — Referral pilot with licensed companies
Legal review of the referral model, signed commission agreements (start with the Sultanahmet airport transfer), company licence verification, approved prices, request form with Bosphorly reference codes, manual confirm/decline by the company, first monthly reconciliation.

## Stage 2 — Request and attribution engine
Role-based access, privacy-by-design guest information, company panel for confirm/decline, availability where the company provides it, coded-sales reporting, commission ledger and invoicing, audit logs.

## Stage 3 — Hotel channel and loyalty
Hotel QR referral identity, contract-defined hotel shares, verified fulfilled-referral rewards, fraud prevention and partner reconciliation. Taking payments on Bosphorly is considered only as a separate decision after licensing review.

## Stage 4 — Istanbul destination data
Verified public transport updates, accessible route recommendations, curated experiences, structured original guides, editorial process, localized discoverability.

## Stage 5 — Mobile applications
Installable PWA validation first, then native clients if demand justifies. Keep booking and policies consistent across platforms.

All production gates in LEGAL-GATES.md apply before real customer use.
