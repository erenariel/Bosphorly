# Bosphorly roadmap

## Stage 0 — Public transparent prototype (current)
Mobile-first arrival planner and supplier research links, public product vision, synthetic partner dashboard and basic Istanbul content. No real transaction capability.

## Stage 1 — Pilot with licensed providers
Supplier/partner verification, signed agreements, destination and hotel geocoding, zone-based quoted fares, confirmed product details, cancellation terms, multilingual disclosure review.

## Stage 2 — Reservation transaction engine
Role-based access, privacy-by-design guest information, real inventory, supplier acknowledgement, card payment via PCI-compliant hosted provider, confirmation/voucher, cancellation/refunds, audit logs.

## Stage 3 — Distribution and loyalty
Hotel QR referral identity, contract-defined commission ledger, invoice/tax treatment, verified fulfilled-sales rewards, fraud prevention and partner reconciliation.

## Stage 4 — Istanbul destination data
Verified public transport updates, accessible route recommendations, curated experiences, structured original guides, editorial process, localized discoverability.

## Stage 5 — Mobile applications
Installable PWA validation first, then native clients if demand justifies. Keep booking and policies consistent across platforms.

All production gates in LEGAL-GATES.md apply before real customer use.
