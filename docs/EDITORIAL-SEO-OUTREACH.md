# Editorial visibility and link earning
Updated 2026-11-25. This is a research shortlist, not an endorsement, partnership or acquired backlink.
## Pitch after deployment
Offer a useful, free resource for travelers: multilingual IST/SAW airport checklist, three-day Istanbul guide and Bosphorus comparison. Share stable canonical links after verifying live content. Ask for editorial review, corrections or inclusion only if useful to the publisher's audience. Do not ask for a specific anchor or ranking guarantee.

## Outreach example (English)
Subject: Multilingual Istanbul arrival checklist for your readers

Hello, we are developing Bosphorly, an Istanbul travel-request service with a multilingual assistant. Our airport arrival guide helps visitors distinguish IST and SAW, compare rail and private transport, and prepare pickup details. If relevant to your readers, we would welcome your editorial feedback or a reference to the resource. There is no obligation, payment or link exchange. We can correct any factual issue you identify. Thank you, Bosphorly Travel.
## Controls
- Verify public contact and editorial fit, personalize one message per organization; no mass automation or fabricated endorsements.
- Disclose any commercial relationship; use rel=sponsored for paid/affiliate links (or nofollow where appropriate). Do not buy followed links, exchange links at scale, or submit spam to forums.
- Track prospect, contact date, reply, source URL, target URL, link attribute, referral sessions and leads. Mark a backlink earned only after visiting its actual source URL. Use Search Console Links as a second check; it is not exhaustive.
- Maintain clear author identity, publication/review dates and official transport sources. Check actual prices and current timetables, not fictional firsthand claims.
- Google references: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features and https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies
