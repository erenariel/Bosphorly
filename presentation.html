TRAVEL · DISCOVER · CONNECT

Public concept, not a live travel agency. No current booking, payment, supplier availability or verified partner integrations.

THE OPPORTUNITY

Guests want clarity.
Partners need a better channel.

Paper brochures, fragmented provider sites and manual hotel recommendations make comparing transfer and tour conditions difficult. Bosphorly proposes one approachable mobile experience with transparently identified operators and terms.

01 / VISITOR

Plan without friction

Find an airport transfer enquiry, explore Istanbul, compare categories and get answers in a familiar language.

02 / HOTEL

Earn visible attribution

Recommend eligible experiences by link or QR, track contract-defined commissions and reduce manual disputes.

03 / SUPPLIER

Reach qualified demand

Offer confirmed products through a controlled catalog, clear availability and documented policies.

HOW THE PLATFORM WILL WORK

Discovery and trusted intermediation.

Commercial transactions will only become available once contracts, supplier verification, consumer disclosures, secure payments and backend booking confirmation exist.

OUR DIFFERENCE

Content that earns trust before conversion.

Knowledge and booking should reinforce each other. Every transport fact needs an official source; every product needs a real provider, inclusions, cancellation terms and correct pricing.

Mobile by default

Large touch targets, short flows, multilingual design and progressive details.

Both sides of Istanbul

Byzantine and Ottoman heritage, European and Asian neighborhoods, transit and contemporary culture.

Search-ready content

Original editorial guides, semantics, structured metadata, localized pages and verified source links.

B2B transparency

Consentful referrals, traceable fulfillment and contract-defined commissions, not hidden margins.

THE ROAD AHEAD

Start with Istanbul.
Build for the world.

First validate the Sultanahmet/Fatih pilot and bring licensed suppliers aboard. Then improve real-world routing, inventory, secure checkout, multi-language support and destination expansion.
