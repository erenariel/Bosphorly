Istanbul Travel Guide

Start with the geography

Istanbul stretches across Europe and Asia, divided by the Bosphorus. Historic districts and modern neighborhoods feel distinct. Choose where to stay based on the experiences you plan to visit.

Heritage in context

Istanbul includes Byzantine, Ottoman and modern histories. Plan the Historic Peninsula, Galata and the Asian-side waterfront as different experiences rather than trying to see everything in one day.

Transport basics

Use the official Metro Istanbul network map for metro and tram lines and verify ferry service through the relevant operator. Airport rail connections differ between IST and SAW.

Before you book

Confirm tour duration, language, meeting point, accessibility, inclusions, cancellation terms and provider identity directly before paying.

How should I spend my first three days in Istanbul?

For a first visit, group nearby sights rather than crossing the city repeatedly.

  1. Day 1 — Historic Peninsula: plan Sultanahmet, the Hippodrome and nearby museums. Check tickets and opening times before leaving.
  2. Day 2 — Galata and the Bosphorus: explore Galata and Karaköy, then choose a ferry or sightseeing cruise depending on your time and weather.
  3. Day 3 — Asian side: take a ferry to Kadıköy or Üsküdar; leave time for the return trip and your airport transfer.

Where should I stay?

Sultanahmet is convenient for historic sights, Karaköy and Galata for waterfront walks and connections, and Kadıköy for an Asian-side neighborhood experience. Choose based on your mobility, airport and planned activities, not just distance on a map.

Plan with the airport arrival guide, Bosphorus cruise guide and public transport guide. Ask Mimo for an itinerary adapted to your dates and interests.
