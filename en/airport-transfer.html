Istanbul Airport Transfers from IST and Sabiha Gökçen (SAW)

Compare metro and private transfers from Istanbul Airport (IST) and Sabiha Gökçen (SAW). Check pickup details and request a transfer to your hotel.

Know your airport

IST (Istanbul Airport) lies northwest of central Istanbul. SAW (Sabiha Gökçen) lies on the Asian side. Check your flight confirmation; the airports are not interchangeable.

Public rail options

M11 is the relevant airport metro corridor for IST and M4 serves Sabiha Gökçen. Confirm current stations, transfers, luggage policies and hours with official operators.

Private transfer checklist

A reliable transfer quotation should state the terminal meeting point, passenger and luggage capacity, child-seat availability, flight-delay policy, final price and cancellation conditions.

Request an airport transfer to your hotel

Have your airport code, arrival date, hotel address and passenger count ready. If you need a child seat or have oversized luggage, mention it when asking for a quote.

Request an Istanbul airport transfer. Check the pickup point and total fare when your request is confirmed.

Quick answers for Istanbul airport arrivals

How much is a private transfer to Sultanahmet?

Bosphorly lists €60 from IST and €65 from SAW, per vehicle for one way to Sultanahmet. Other destinations, vehicle sizes and special requirements need a separate quote. These are Bosphorly's displayed prices, not a citywide taxi fare; confirm the final quote before booking.

Which option suits a late arrival or a family?

Check the final metro departure and your hotel's walking distance. With children or several bags, ask for a vehicle with suitable capacity and a child seat if needed. A prearranged ride may reduce transfers, but is subject to availability and road traffic.

Is a submitted request a confirmed ride?

No. Wait for the provider's confirmation of the pickup point, vehicle, total fare and terms. No payment is taken on the Bosphorly website.
