ISTANBUL VISITOR GUIDE

Istanbul Public Transport

Understand the metro, tram, Marmaray and ferry network before traveling.

Independent editorial preview. Tours, prices and schedules are not live.

Historic Peninsula

The T1 tram serves central historic districts, including Sultanahmet and Eminönü. Station access and service can change.

Both sides of the city

Marmaray provides a cross-Bosphorus rail connection. Ferries are another useful option between waterfront neighborhoods; check current schedules.

Airports

IST and SAW require different travel plans. Avoid confusing the old Atatürk Airport metro station with the operating Istanbul Airport (IST).

Official maps

Metro Istanbul publishes route maps and service updates. This website does not provide live departure predictions.
