Historic Peninsula

Sultanahmet brings together architectural layers from Byzantine Constantinople and Ottoman Istanbul. Give museums, monuments and public spaces time rather than rushing.

Hagia Sophia

Its complex history spans an imperial church, mosque, museum era and present-day mosque use. Visitor access rules can change; consult official sources.

Topkapı and the Grand Bazaar

Topkapı Palace illustrates the Ottoman court period, while the Grand Bazaar reflects Istanbul's longstanding commercial networks.

Visit responsibly

Respect worship, dress and photography rules; confirm opening times and any ticket requirements with the relevant institution.
